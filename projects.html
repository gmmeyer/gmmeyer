Personal Projects

Lately I've been training small models from scratch at home — a language model, a world model, a music model, and a diffusion model — and shipping the ones that are fun to play with straight into the browser.

Cicero · a Latin LLM in your browser

2026
  • A ~111M-parameter Latin language model trained from a random init on a ~466M-token corpus — no pretrained backbone, no English base.
  • Runs entirely client-side: the int8-quantized model downloads once and generates via ONNX Runtime Web. No server, no API.

The Dreaming Bird · a transformer that is Flappy Bird

2026
  • An ~11M-parameter world model that emits the next frame's state from the current frame and your input — no physics engine at runtime; the model is the physics.
  • It learns the hidden game physics from data (one-step bird position 98.6% exact, collisions within ±1 frame) and stays stable through 5+ pipes of free autoregressive rollout.
  • Playable in the browser via onnxruntime-web (WebGPU with a WASM fallback), in the lineage of World Models, Othello-GPT, and GameNGen.

midigpt · expressive solo piano generation

2026
  • An autoregressive transformer (~27M params; RoPE, RMSNorm, SwiGLU) that generates expressive solo piano, trained on the MAESTRO dataset with Music-Transformer-style event tokenization.
